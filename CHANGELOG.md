# Changelog

## 1.6.0
Built from the bank's risk framework documents (charters, ERM framework, risk appetite framework, the policies and manuals of the five sections, the ICAAP package, the continuity plan, a circular implementation plan) and its model quarterly board report, which are kept as the standard for the platform's outputs. The documents themselves are **not** in the repository: the platform holds the methodology in generic wording and the bank loads its own files on its own server.
- **Framework documents** (`/policies/framework`): document register on the seven-level hierarchy (charters, risk appetite, ERM framework, policies, manuals, methodologies and tools, plans and reports) with classification (public / internal / confidential / secret), approving body, parent document and supervising section; import of Word files (control table read automatically; several files at once, or `bin/import-documents.php` on the server), new versions kept, manuals linked to their policy, cited codes checked and **missing references** listed; confidential and secret files downloadable only by risk, compliance and internal audit; each import indexed in the reference library (restricted when confidential).
- **Unified taxonomy and scale**: risk categories carry the ERM group and supervising section (continuity and capital added); the score bands become 1–4 low, 5–9 medium, 10–16 high, 17–25 critical (applied only where the old default bands were still in place).
- **Risk-function workbench** (`/rm`): catalogue of 58 recurring procedures of the five sections with frequency and approver, generated work calendar and My tasks; independent opinions (no objection / conditional / reservation) with four-eyes review, issue by the risk director, the approving body's decision and automatic escalation of contrary decisions; limit breaches and exceptions (no exception against a regulatory limit; regulatory or material breach escalated); unified escalation log to the risk committee chair (CEO informed unless the case concerns them); Word exports.
- **Risk appetite framework** (`/appetite/framework`): board-level metrics on the KRI engine with appetite level, warning limit and maximum, formula, source and frequency; standard template of 49 metrics in nine categories created uncalibrated; readings refused until calibrated; amber creates a warning notice, red escalates; notice page and Word notice; green/amber/red dashboard with trend.
- **ICAAP** (`/icaap`): annual cycle with nine phases and checklists, capital inputs reconciled with the last regulatory return, materiality of 16 risks (impact ≥ 4 or score ≥ 10), Pillar 1 + Pillar 2 + management buffer, three-year stress paths for three scenarios with target / warning / trigger / minimum zones, assumptions log and data requests, alerts and escalation, report sections, declaration → committee → board → submission, lock after submission, print and Word report. Calculations match the bank's ICAAP workbook on test data.
- **Quarterly board risk report** (`/board-reports`): the model report's eleven sections with cover letter, reading note, dashboard tiles (suggested from board metrics, events, escalations and the latest ICAAP ratio), risk profile, key messages, data sources and references (the framework documents in one click), observations, priority risks and the operational events log read live from the registers, data-quality findings, proposed decisions (information / approval / direction), recommendations by department with priority and duration; risk committee and board approval; on approval decisions become board decisions and recommendations become owned, dated actions; locked when approved; print and Word.
- **Supporting registers** (`/registers`): model register, data delivery schedule (late receipt flagged), maturity assessment on the framework's eight axes and five levels, temporary security acceptances (expiry escalated by the daily job), emerging risks, regulatory requirements matrix per circular (risk-function role, tool/output, indicators, compliance summary), committee annual plan with standing agenda and a private session with the risk director at every meeting.
- Help topics for the six new areas; My tasks and the daily job cover the new items (`bin/notify.php` prints `rm_tasks` and `security_expired`).
- Migrations 024 to 029. Permissions `rm.view`, `rm.work`, `rm.approve`, `rm.breaches`, `rm.respond`, `icaap.view`, `icaap.manage`. 1620 automated checks.

## 1.5.0
- **AI compliance assistant** (`/assistant`, every role): answers questions on AML/CFT, compliance, risk, internal audit, Sharia governance and continuity **only from the reference library**, with a numbered citation for every point and a link to each original source. Two modes: *library mode* (default, nothing leaves the server; returns the best matching passages) and *Claude mode* (the administrator enables it once an API key is in the config file; Claude writes the answer from the retrieved passages only). The assistant is read-only (no tools, cannot see or change records), account/phone/card/national numbers, IBANs and e-mails are removed from questions automatically, prompts treat sources and questions as data (prompt-injection rules), answers are rendered as escaped text, every question is logged and audited, and questions per user per hour are limited.
- **Reference library** (`/assistant/library`): 28 built-in sources and 57 bilingual passages — FATF Recommendations, risk-based approach, PEP and high-risk-jurisdiction guidance, UN sanctions, Basel (AML/CFT, compliance function, corporate governance, Core Principles 2024, operational risk and resilience, internal audit), Wolfsberg, MENAFATF, Libyan Law 2/2005 and Decree 300, Law 3/2014, Banking Law 1/2005 with Law 46/2012, Law 1/2013 on riba, Central Bank of Libya, ISO 31000/22301/27001, COSO, IIA Global Internal Audit Standards, AAOIFI, IFSB, and the platform's own methods. Each passage is a summary with a link to the original. Compliance and risk add the bank's circulars, policies and procedures (optionally restricted), or add a registered circular in one click; documents can be withdrawn and restored.
- **Search** in PHP (no search server): Arabic normalisation and light stemming, Arabic↔English term bridge, BM25 ranking, module boost; laws and standards rank ahead of platform notes.
- **Where it appears**: "Related references and standards" panels on AML, internal audit, compliance, Sharia, BIA, controls, risks, KRIs, incidents, RCSA and governance pages; "Ask the assistant" on AML customers, audit findings and compliance obligations, sending only de-identified fields that the user sees before asking.
- **Administration** (`/admin/assistant`): switch on/off, mode, model (`claude-opus-5-5` default, Sonnet or Haiku), reasoning effort, hourly limit, connection test, library reload, 30-day usage. The API key is read only from the config file or the `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` environment variable.
- Migration 023. Permissions `ai.use` (all roles) and `kb.manage` (risk, compliance, admin).

## 1.4.0
- **Internal audit** (`/ia`): audit universe scored on six risk factors (department residual risk, materiality, regulatory exposure, complexity, time since last audit, last rating) with audit cycles by priority; annual plan built from the ranking, approval by the audit committee recorded and the plan locked; engagements planned → fieldwork → reporting → issued; workpapers with sample, exceptions, conclusion and evidence, reviewed by a second auditor (maker-checker); findings written as condition / criteria / cause / effect / recommendation, kept as drafts until the report is issued, then followed with management (response within 14 days, agreed action and date, implementation, auditor verification or return, counted extensions).
- **Anti-money laundering** (`/aml`): risk factors in four dimensions (customers, products and services, delivery channels, geographic areas) with illustrative levels to be reviewed by the bank; customer rating from weighted dimensions plus hard rules (PEP or sanctions match = high; high-risk type or country never below medium), due-diligence level and review cycle; manual override with reason; enterprise-wide view with inherent risk, control effectiveness from linked controls and residual risk; AML reports (STR confidential to the AML team, CTR, periodic, EWRA) with due dates and submission records; CSV export.
- **T24 integration** (`/admin/integrations`): customers and loss-ledger entries loaded from a T24 export file (CSV, pipe, semicolon, tab or JSON) or from the T24 REST API (IRIS-style paging, basic or bearer authentication, encrypted secret), with configurable field mapping, value mapping through the AML factors, all-or-nothing validation, run log and a cron job `bin/t24-sync.php`.
- My tasks, e-mail digests and the dashboard include the new items; help topics and role quick-starts updated.
- Migrations 020 to 022.
